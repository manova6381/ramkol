FMCG

FMCG Products Suppliers in India

Ramkol is actively engaged in the export of a wide range of fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), featuring products and brands that are widely recognized and sold both in India and internationally. As a direct global export partner for leading companies such as Unilever and others, we are proud to be one of the key players in the FMCG export industry. Our vision is to establish Ramkol as one of the largest and most trusted FMCG exporters in India.

Fast-moving consumer goods (FMCGs), also known as consumer packaged goods, are low-cost items with high turnover and widespread distribution. These include non-durable household essentials such as frozen foods, snacks, canned beverages, over-the-counter medications, personal care items, cosmetics, toiletries, dry goods, and more. India is home to numerous FMCG companies offering a diverse range of products catering to a variety of consumer needs. In the global market, FMCG products are among the most competitive and demand-driven, making the industry dynamic and fast-paced.
