Products Description

Dry Red Chilli

Ramkol Products is manufacturer and exporter of Chilly.Ramkol manufactures and exports Chilly as per US FDA and ASTA quality standards.Chilly is known as “Mirch” in India. Chilly is used to enhance pungent taste of food. India is largest producer and consumer of Chilly in world. The major Chilly producing states of India are Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Orissa, West Bengal, Maharastra, Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat. Ramkol manufactures and exports Chilly Teja With Stem, Chilly Teja Stemless, Chilly S4 With Stem and Chilly S4 Stemless.

Chilli Powder

Ramkol is manufacturer and exporter of Chilly Powder, Ground Chilly.Ramkol manufactures and exports Chilly Powder, Ground Chilly as per US FDA and ASTA quality standards.Chilly Powder is well known as Lal Mirch Powder.
