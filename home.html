ABOUT US

RAMKOL Global Trading, Distribution & Consulting

With 20+ years of experience, we are a trusted Indian company specializing in General Trading, Import-Export, and Global Distribution of high-quality FMCG products. We connect reliable Indian and international brands with markets across the Middle East, Africa, and Europe.

We offer a secure platform for the global supply of goods, working closely with producers, wholesalers, and importers to build strong, transparent partnerships. Our goal is to bridge the gap between buyers and sellers worldwide through efficient, end-to-end trade solutions.

As one of India’s leading FMCG exporters, we provide products from top brands like Unilever, P&G, Tata, ITC, Nestlé, Godrej, and also offer OEM and private label services.

Driven by a skilled team and a robust supply network, we ensure quality, compliance, and customer satisfaction at every stage.
